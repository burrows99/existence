# existence

A universe you can zoom into anywhere, where everything is genuinely made of the level below it.

**Live: https://burrows99.github.io/existence/**

Scroll on anything to zoom into it. It starts at the observable universe, 93 billion light-years of galaxies along a cosmic web. Inside is the Milky Way, a hundred billion stars each made of plasma; 26,000 light-years out is the solar system as it is today, the Sun a ball of protons and electrons and the planets at their real positions; the Earth has an iron core, a mantle and crust of real mineral lattices, salty oceans, air, and on Santa Cruz in the Galápagos a meadow where creatures evolve (the links at the bottom take you there). Each creature is steered by its own small neural network, lives on the grass it grazes or the smaller creatures it catches, shows its energy as a life bar, and breeds when well fed: its young inherit its brain, size and colour with mutations, so natural selection runs as you watch. Click one to follow it. Every creature is skin, fat, muscle and a brain of cells, every tuft of grass is grass cells; a cell's membranes are real lipid bilayers, its nucleus is full of DNA, its mitochondria have their own; the cytosol has proteins, ATP, glucose and water, all real structures with every hydrogen. Keep scrolling: a protein is its 2,541 atoms, each atom dissolves into its electron cloud sampled from real orbitals with the nucleus at the centre, the nucleus is its protons and neutrons, and each of those is three quarks. Every spot is a link.

Nothing is stored. Every object regenerates the same contents from a seed when you look at it, so a cell of 10¹⁴ atoms costs nothing until you zoom into one of them. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for how it works and what comes next.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev     # http://localhost:5173/existence/
npm test        # checks the orbital maths, the orbits, and that evolved brains out-forage random ones
```
