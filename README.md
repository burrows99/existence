# existence

A universe you can zoom into anywhere, where everything is genuinely made of the level below it.

**Live: https://burrows99.github.io/existence/**

Scroll on anything to zoom into it. It starts at the observable universe, 93 billion light-years of galaxies along a cosmic web. Inside is the Milky Way, a hundred billion stars each made of plasma; 26,000 light-years out is the solar system as it is today, the Sun a ball of protons and electrons and the planets at their real positions; the Earth has an iron core, a mantle and crust of real mineral lattices, salty oceans, air, and a person standing on a continent (the links at the bottom take you there). Skin, fat, muscle, bone and organs are packed with cells of their kind; a cell's membranes are real lipid bilayers, its nucleus is full of DNA, its mitochondria have their own; the cytosol has proteins, ATP, glucose and water, all real structures with every hydrogen. Keep scrolling: a protein is its 2,541 atoms, each atom dissolves into its electron cloud sampled from real orbitals with the nucleus at the centre, the nucleus is its protons and neutrons, and each of those is three quarks. Every spot is a link.

Nothing is stored. Every object regenerates the same contents from a seed when you look at it, so a cell of 10¹⁴ atoms costs nothing until you zoom into one of them. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for how it works and what comes next.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev     # http://localhost:5173/existence/
npm test        # checks the orbital maths
```
